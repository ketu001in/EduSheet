**Important honesty note**: Aryabhata (476 CE), Brahmagupta (598-668 CE),
and Bhaskara II (1114-1185 CE) all lived many centuries before
photography existed -- no contemporary portrait of any of them survives,
and in most cases none was ever made. The images used here are a modern
statue, a modern relief sculpture, and a modern illustrated portrait
respectively: artistic tributes, not likenesses. Only Ramanujan
(1887-1920) lived in the photographic era, so his image is a genuine
historical photograph. The in-app copy for each figure says this
explicitly rather than implying otherwise.

Hotspot positions and all explanatory text are original, hand-verified
